The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants (en-GB, nl-NL, de-DE and the rest) book 3 October and get an invite for 10 March. This is what #4471 says, and the queued fix is for this.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Tenants on booking-v3 write the day first. Their people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Fixing the parser does not fix this: the hint has to match the tenant’s locale.

one-day: the date moves by exactly one day. Tuesday becomes Monday, every booking in June is off by a day, a client in Lisbon and one in Toronto get different days for one meeting, an invite arrives before the booking. Day and month are not swapped. This looks like a time zone or daylight saving conversion. The desk noted these reports as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), but causes.ts has no cause for them, so they stay counted under parser by tenant. They need their own fix all the same.

Why it was merged as one

  • Duplicates were merged by subject line (decision 0001). Every report about a wrong date has a similar subject, whatever caused it.
  • The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. It disagrees with the tenant for many reports, so counting by tag gives the wrong split.
  • The export comes in overlapping pages: some reports appear twice. Count by id.

For the desk

  • Before merging into a big ticket, check the cause by tenant and channel, not the subject.
  • When a report does not fit a known cause, give it its own ticket, even if it is the only one.
